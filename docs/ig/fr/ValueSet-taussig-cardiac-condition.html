ValueSet: TAUSSIG — CardiacCondition (lésions résiduelles/acquises/post-chir) (Expérimental)

URL officielle: https://taussig/fhir/ValueSet/taussig-cardiac-condition Version: 0.1.0
Draft à date du 2026-11-09 Nom computable: TaussigCardiacCondition

Working set des conditions cardiaques évolutives de la POC (distinct du diagnostic congénital figé) : 61 lésions résiduelles/acquises codées en IPCCC, plus 13 états d'étape chirurgicale codés en ECHSA — l'IPCCC ne code pas l'état « status post » d'un montage (Fontan, Glenn, MBTS…), d'où un ValueSet multi-systèmes. ICD-10 = donnée dérivée (crossmap IPCCC). Le typage residual/acquired/post-chir est un choix clinicien à la saisie (CardiacCondition.condition_type), pas une propriété du code.

References

Ce jeu de valeurs n'est pas utilisé ici ; il peut être utilisé autre part (par exemple dans les spécifications et / ou implémentations qui utilisent ce contenu)

    SP-2010-4870 Intervention de Norwood The Norwood operation is synonymous with the term 'Norwood (Stage 1)' and is defined as an aortopulmonary connection and neoaortic arch construction resulting in univentricular physiology and pulmonary blood flow controlled with a calibrated systemic-to-pulmonary artery shunt, or a right ventricle to pulmonary artery conduit, or rarely, a cavopulmonary connection. When coding the procedure 'Norwood procedure', the primary procedure of the operation should be 'Norwood procedure'. The second procedure (Procedure 2 after the Norwood procedure) must then document the source of pulmonary blood flow and be chosen from the following eight choices: 1. Shunt, Systemic to pulmonary, Modified Blalock- Taussig Shunt (MBTS) 2. Shunt, Systemic to pulmonary, Central (from aorta or to main pulmonary artery) 3. Shunt, Systemic to pulmonary, Other 4. Conduit placement, RV to PA 5. Bidirectional cavopulmonary anastomosis (BDCPA) (bidirectional Glenn) 6. Glenn (unidirectional cavopulmonary anastomosis) (unidirectional Glenn) 7. Bilateral bidirectional cavopulmonary anastomosis (BBDCPA) (bilateral bidirectional Glenn) 8. HemiFontan
    SP-2010-5680 Anastomose cavopulmonaire unidirectionnelle (Glenn unidirectionnel) Superior vena cava to ipsilateral pulmonary artery anastomosis (i.e., LSVC to LPA, RSVC to RPA).
    SP-2010-5670 Anastomose cavopulmonaire bidirectionnelle (Glenn bidirectionnel) Superior vena cava to pulmonary artery anastomosis allowing flow to both pulmonary arteries with an end-to-side superior vena-to-pulmonary artery anastomosis.
    SP-2010-5690 Anastomose cavopulmonaire bidirectionnelle bilatérale Bilateral superior vena cava-to-pulmonary artery anastomoses (requires bilateral SVCs).
    SP-2010-5000 Fontan (TCPC), conduit extracardiaque, fenestré Total cavopulmonary connection using an external conduit to connect the infradiaphragmatic systemic venous return to the pulmonary artery, with fenestration.
    SP-2010-5010 Fontan (TCPC), conduit extracardiaque, non fenestré Total cavopulmonary connection using an external conduit to connect the infradiaphragmatic systemic venous return to the pulmonary artery, with no fenestration.
    SP-2010-4970 Fontan (TCPC), tunnel latéral, fenestré Total cavopulmonary connection using an intraatrial lateral tunnel construction, with fenestration.
    SP-2010-4980 Fontan (TCPC), tunnel latéral, non fenestré Total cavopulmonary connection using an intraatrial lateral tunnel construction, with no fenestration.
    SP-2010-4520 Réparation à un ventricule et demi Partial biventricular repair; includes intracardiac repair with bidirectional cavopulmonary anastomosis to volume unload a small ventricle or poorly functioning ventricle.
    SP-2010-5710 Palliation, autre Any other palliative procedure not specifically listed.
